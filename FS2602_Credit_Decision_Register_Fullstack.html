Rule versions

Rules are data, not code. Every application is scored against whichever version was effective on its scoring date.

Publish a new version

No deployment — this becomes the active rulebook for anything scored from its effective date onward. Weights should sum to roughly 1.0.

Hash-chained log

Every scored decision is appended with a SHA-256 hash over its record plus the previous record's hash. Changing anything after the fact breaks the chain from that point forward.

Historical replay

Pick a past application and a rulebook version. Replaying re-runs the exact same scoring function against the stored inputs — a real submission needs this to return a byte-identical result years later.
